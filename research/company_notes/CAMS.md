# Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS) — Company Note

**Ticker:** CAMS | **Price (29 Oct 2026):** ₹697 | **Market cap:** ₹17,300 crore | **EV:** ₹16,394 crore

## Business

CAMS is India's largest mutual-fund registrar and transfer agent (RTA). It processes subscriptions, redemptions, SIPs, folios, distributor commissions and compliance for AMCs. Revenue is primarily asset/folio-linked and recurring.

- FY26 revenue ₹1,516 crore; MF segment 86%, non-MF 14%.
- Q1 FY27 revenue ₹395 crore, EBITDA ₹182 crore (46.1% margin), PAT ₹127.1 crore.
- Serviced MF AUM ₹56 lakh crore in Q1 FY27, a 67.2% RTA share (Q4 FY26 ~68%).
- Non-MF revenue grew 28.4% YoY and was 14.9% of revenue.
- FY27 guidance: revenue +13%, EBITDA +16%.
- Net cash: ₹971.45 crore cash at 30 June 2026; FY26 cash ₹854.45 crore; ₹64 crore debt at FY26 (likely lease liabilities). Model net cash is ₹906 crore.

## Moat

- **Oligopoly:** CAMS and KFin dominate MF RTA; CAMS has roughly two-thirds share.
- **High switching costs:** AMC investor records, distributor mappings, compliance history and workflows are embedded. Migration is operationally risky and requires approvals.
- **Scale:** 46% EBITDA margin, 38.7% average-equity ROE, 42.2% pre-tax ROCE, 110% pre-tax CFO/EBITDA (86% after tax) in FY26.
- **But:** fees are visible and politically sensitive; AMCs face TER pressure. The moat protects retention more than it protects pricing.

## Growth

- MF AUM growth from SIPs, new investors and financialisation of savings.
- Non-MF services (insurance, AIF, PMS, alternatives) growing faster off a small base.
- FY27E revenue +13%, EPS ₹22.3; FY28E EPS ₹25.1.
- Operating leverage is real but partly offset by technology, compliance and cyber spend.

## Financial quality

- FY26 CFO ₹584 crore, FCF ₹444 crore, FCF/EBITDA 65%; net cash.
- FCF yield 2.6%; P/FCF 39.0x — the best cash-flow valuation support in the universe.
- Working capital is negative/low; receivables 14 days; capital intensity low.
- Dividend payout 65%; no leverage; high return on capital.

## Thesis

**Bull:** AUM growth stays in double digits, CAMS defends ~67% share, non-MF grows >25%, margins hold at 46–47%, and the stock re-rates from 28x FY28E toward 33–35x. The balance sheet supports higher dividends or bolt-on acquisitions.

**Bear:** A 20% equity drawdown cuts AUM and revenue, fee pressure compresses margins, and the multiple de-rates to 28x. The equity-drawdown shock alone reduces FY27 revenue ~13% and EPS ~14%; the joint adverse case sets a ₹532 target, ~36% below the scenario base target.

**Exactly three falsifiers (mapped to the exit triggers):**
1. **Share loss:** MF RTA share falls below 63% for two consecutive quarters, or a top-5 AMC publicly migrates a material mandate.
2. **Fee/regulatory impairment:** SEBI/AMFI imposes a binding RTA fee cap or common platform that cuts MF revenue yield >10%, or removes the asset-based fee model.
3. **Valuation/earnings break:** forward P/E exceeds 45x while forward revenue growth is below 8%, or the stock reaches ₹950 with no upward revision to FY28 EPS.

## Verdict

**BUY.** CAMS offers the best combination of recurring revenue, market share, returns, net cash and starting valuation. The DCF (₹712) is close to the current price, while forward multiples (₹831) capture the 12-month earnings roll. The blended ₹800 target implies +16.6% total return, with a credible −23.6% bear case — the best risk/reward in the universe. The key risk is fee regulation, not volume collapse.
