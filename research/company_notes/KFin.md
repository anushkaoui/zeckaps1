# KFin Technologies Limited — Company Note

**Ticker:** KFINTECH | **Price (29 Oct 2026):** ₹860 | **Market cap:** ₹14,878 crore | **EV:** ₹14,433 crore

## Business

KFin is India's #2 mutual-fund RTA and a diversified financial-services platform. It provides MF investor services, issuer services (registrar/transfer agent for companies, IPOs, corporate actions), international investor solutions (Malaysia, Philippines, Hong Kong, and the Ascent acquisition) and adjacent services.

Q2 FY27 revenue was ₹401.8 crore, EBITDA ₹161.0 crore (40.1% margin), PAT ₹100.3 crore, EPS ₹5.78. H1 FY27 revenue was ₹758.3 crore and PAT ₹175.6 crore. Segment mix in Q2:

- Domestic MF: ₹232.5 crore (58%)
- International & other investor solutions: ₹110.8 crore (28%; international sub-segment ~20%)
- Issuer: ₹50.9 crore (13%)
- Other services (allied/GBS/OPE): remaining balance

Domestic MF AAUM market share was 32.5% in Q2 FY27, with a similar SIP-book share. FY26 revenue was ₹1,301 crore and PAT ₹344 crore.

## Moat

- **#2 RTA with high switching costs:** embedded AMC workflows, investor records and compliance history.
- **Diversified revenue:** domestic MF, issuer and international services reduce dependence on one fee pool.
- **International growth:** Ascent and new licenses (e.g., Thailand) add a second leg.
- **But:** smaller share than CAMS, lower margins, and a more complex acquisition/integration profile. The moat is narrower and less proven through a cycle.

## Growth

- FY27 guidance: revenue +18–20%, domestic MF +14–15%, international +30%.
- Q2 FY27 revenue grew 30% YoY; margins recovered to 40.1% after a weak Q1 (34%).
- Base case: FY27 revenue +20%, EPS ₹24.1; FY28 EPS ₹28.3.
- International and issuer services are the main incremental growth drivers; domestic MF remains the profit anchor.

## Financial quality

- FY26 CFO ₹370 crore, FCF ₹259 crore, FCF/EBITDA 49%; net cash.
- Average-equity ROE 22.3%; pre-tax ROCE 25.7% — lower than CAMS/BSE but improving.
- P/FCF 57.4x, FCF yield 1.7%; working capital has become less negative after the Ascent consolidation.
- Promoter holding fell to 14.06% in September 2026, a governance/overhang consideration.

## Thesis

**Bull:** International/issuer growth sustains 18–20% revenue growth, margins return to 41–42%, and the stock re-rates toward 40x. The Ascent acquisition proves accretive and new licenses scale.

**Bear:** Domestic MF share stagnates, international integration costs/margins disappoint, and the multiple de-rates to 27x. A 30% derivatives fall has a negligible direct impact; a 20% equity drawdown cuts revenue ~4% and EPS ~4.6%. The larger risk is execution and multiple compression.

**Falsifiers:**
- Domestic MF AAUM share falls below 30%.
- International revenue growth falls below 15% for two quarters.
- EBITDA margin stays below 38% for two quarters.
- Promoter sells down further without a credible strategic buyer.

## Verdict

**Reject.** KFin is a credible #2 with improving growth and a lower P/E than CAMS, but it lacks CAMS's share, margin, ROE and balance-sheet cushion. The base-case total return (+10.1%) is below CAMS, and the bear case (−38%) is deeper. The stock is a watchlist candidate, not the winner.
