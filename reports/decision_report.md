**Execution rules:**

- Do not chase above ₹760 before full deployment: at ₹760 the upside to the ₹800 target is only 5.3%, below the required margin of safety.
- If price >₹800 before tranche 3, stop buying and reassess; the target is already reached.
- Verify the actual 20-day ADV before execution. The ₹50 crore ADV is a planning assumption, not an exchange-verified figure. If ADV is lower, extend the build period; do not exceed 15% participation.

- Single-stock cap 5%; review if mark-to-market weight exceeds 5.5%.

**Position sizing logic:** 4% of the sleeve is meaningful but not concentrated enough to create liquidity or governance risk. The 5% cap limits single-name drawdown to ₹25 crore; at the bear-case target (₹532), the loss on a fully built ₹20 crore position is ~₹4.7 crore, or ~0.9% of the ₹500 crore sleeve.

## 5. Exactly three exit/thesis-review triggers

1. **Market-share loss:** CAMS MF RTA share falls below **63% for two consecutive quarters**, or a top-5 AMC publicly migrates a material mandate. Action: exit or reduce to zero after verification.
2. **Fee/regulatory impairment:** SEBI/AMFI implements a binding RTA fee cap or common platform that reduces CAMS's MF revenue yield by **more than 10%**, or removes the asset-based fee model. Action: re-underwrite; exit if the target falls below ₹697.
3. **Valuation/earnings break:** forward P/E exceeds **45x** while forward revenue growth is below **8%**, or the stock reaches **₹950** with no upward revision to FY28 EPS. Action: take profits/review.

These are the only three triggers. They are measurable and tied to the thesis, not to price noise.

## 6. Rejection analysis

### BSE — strongest bull case, weakest risk asymmetry

**Bull case:** derivatives share gains, 20%+ EPS growth, 50x multiple → ₹4,655 (+33% total). **Why reject:** the stock is at 50.6x TTM and 40.3x EV/EBITDA, with 74–85% of revenue from transaction charges and a large share from expiry-day derivatives. A 30% derivatives turnover fall cuts FY27 EPS ~28%; the joint adverse case cuts the target ~52%. The base case is −0.9%, and the probability-weighted return is −5.0%. We are not paid for the regulatory tail.

**What would change the view:** SEBI clarifies that no further expiry/position-limit tightening is planned, BSE sustains >60% notional share, and the stock de-rates below 35x FY28E.

### KFin — credible #2, but not enough margin of safety

**Bull case:** international/issuer growth, margin recovery, 42x multiple → ₹1,343 (+58% total). **Why reject:** KFin has 32.5% MF AAUM share, 40.7% FY26 EBITDA margin, 22.3% ROE and 25.7% ROCE — all below CAMS. Its FCF conversion is the lowest (49%), working capital is the heaviest (76 debtor days), and the Ascent integration/promoter sell-down add complexity. Base price upside is +8.6% (+10.0% total), and the bear case is −37.8%. The discount to CAMS is not large enough.

**What would change the view:** two consecutive quarters of >42% EBITDA margin, international growth >20%, and a price below ₹800.

### CDSL — widest moat, worst valuation/growth mix

**Bull case:** demat additions re-accelerate, 55x multiple → ₹1,680 (+32%). **Why reject:** 57x TTM, 55x FY27E, 49.5x FY28E, 77x FCF and a 5.7x PEG, against 9.7% EPS CAGR and a FY26 PAT decline. The base case is −6.1%; even the probability-weighted return is −6.0%. This is a high-quality business priced for perfection.

**What would change the view:** price below ₹950, or demat additions >40 lakh/quarter with positive FY27 EPS growth, or a credible new fee pool.

## 7. What would change the ranking

- **CAMS:** share loss below 63%, a binding fee cap, or a large dilutive acquisition.
- **BSE:** regulatory clarity plus a de-rating to <35x FY28E; or a sustained derivatives share >60% with no new curbs.
- **CDSL:** a 25–30% price correction or a new growth engine.
- **KFin:** margin >42% for two quarters and integration proof.

## 8. Key risks to the decision

1. **Fee regulation** is the main CAMS risk. The base case assumes no binding cap; a cap that cuts MF revenue yield >10% would reduce the target by roughly 15–25%.
2. **Market drawdown** hits CAMS's AUM-linked revenue more than its F&O exposure; the −20% equity shock is the relevant stress.
3. **Q2 FY27 execution** is imminent; the position is staged so only the first tranche is exposed to the print.
4. **Liquidity** is assumed at ₹50 crore ADV; verify before trading.
5. **DCF** is close to the current price; the target relies on the forward multiple, so a de-rating to 28x would imply only ₹705.

## 9. Conclusion

**Buy CAMS, build 4% of the ₹500 crore sleeve in three tranches, target ₹800 (12 months), and use the three triggers above to exit.** The decision is a quality-at-a-reasonable-price call, not a growth-at-any-price call. BSE is the high-beta alternative; KFin is the watchlist name; CDSL is the quality stock to revisit after a de-rating.
