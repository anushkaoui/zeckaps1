## 2. What the numbers say

### Growth vs quality

KFin has the highest forecast EPS CAGR (17.8%), followed by BSE (14.4%), CAMS (13.7%) and CDSL (9.7%). BSE's growth is driven by derivatives volume/share and operating leverage; KFin's by international/issuer expansion and margin recovery. CAMS grows slower but with the highest earnings quality and the widest MF RTA share. CDSL has the weakest growth and the highest multiple — the worst combination.

### Returns and cash conversion

BSE has the highest ROE/ROCE, but its equity base is small relative to the profit pool and its cash flow is volatile (FY25 CFO fell to ₹414 crore from ₹2,643 crore in FY24). CAMS has the most consistent cash conversion: FY26 pre-tax CFO/EBITDA 110% (after-tax 86%), FCF/EBITDA 65%, and a net-cash balance sheet. KFin's cash conversion is lower (FCF/EBITDA 49%) and its working capital has worsened after the Ascent consolidation. CDSL converts cash well (FCF/EBITDA 59%) but is priced at 77x FCF.

### Valuation

CAMS is the cheapest on every cash-flow and earnings multiple except PEG, where KFin is marginally lower. The gap is material:

- CAMS trades at 24.0x EV/EBITDA and 27.7x FY28E EPS, versus BSE at 40.3x/42.5x and CDSL at 43.1x/49.5x.
- CAMS's FCF yield (2.6%) is roughly 45% higher than BSE's (1.8%) and double CDSL's (1.3%).
- BSE's PEG (3.3x) is higher than CAMS's (2.3x), and BSE's growth is far more regulatory-sensitive.
- KFin's PEG is lowest, but its ROE, margin and moat are weaker.

## 4. Falsifiable theses

- **BSE:** If derivatives share and volume growth continue, EPS can compound ~18% and the stock can hold 40x+. Falsified by a structural SEBI restriction or a sustained share loss to NSE.
- **CDSL:** If demat additions stay >30 lakh/quarter and fees are stable, the network deserves a premium. Falsified by slowing account growth or a fee cut.
- **CAMS:** If MF AUM grows low-double-digits and CAMS holds ~65%+ share, EPS can compound ~14% with 46% margins. Falsified by share loss below 63% or a binding fee cap.
- **KFin:** If international/issuer growth stays >15% and margins recover to 41%+, EPS can compound ~18%. Falsified by integration slippage or domestic share loss.

## 6. Conclusion

The comparison supports a **CAMS-first** decision. It is not the fastest grower, but it has the best combination of:

1. A durable oligopoly position with ~67% MF RTA share.
2. Recurring, asset-linked revenue and 46% EBITDA margins.
3. 38.7% ROE, 42.2% ROCE, net cash and 2.6% FCF yield.
4. The lowest EV/EBITDA and FY28 P/E in the group.
5. The shallowest bear case (−23.6%) and the highest probability-weighted return (+15.2%).

BSE is the high-beta alternative; CDSL is quality at an unreasonable price; KFin is a credible watchlist name. The detailed winner model is in `reports/valuation_report.md`.

## 7. Data caveats

- BSE's Q1 FY27 revenue/EBITDA definitions differ between Screener and the company presentation. Its FY26 media release reports ₹5,148 crore total revenue and ₹3,079 crore operating EBITDA versus ₹5,124 crore/₹3,480 crore normalized; the margin comparison is therefore not like-for-like.
- CDSL's revenue mix is approximated from disclosed categories.
- CAMS Q2 FY27 results were not available at the cut-off; Q1 FY27 and guidance are used.
- KFin Q2 FY27 is used; segment totals include other/inter-segment items.
- Net cash for BSE/CDSL/KFin is haircut for restricted/regulatory balances; CAMS uses reported cash less debt.
