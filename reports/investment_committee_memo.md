# Investment Committee Memo — CAMS

**Recommendation:** BUY | **Price:** ₹697 (29 Oct 2026) | **Target:** ₹800 | **Upside:** +14.8% price, +16.6% total | **Position:** 4% of ₹500 cr sleeve (₹20 cr) | **Horizon:** 12 months

## Decision

Buy **Computer Age Management Services (CAMS)**, India's largest mutual-fund RTA, and build a 4% position in three tranches. CAMS offers the best risk-adjusted exposure to Indian capital-market growth: ~67% MF RTA share, 46% EBITDA margins, 38.7% ROE, net cash and the lowest EV/EBITDA in the peer set. The stock is not cheap in absolute terms, but it is cheaper than BSE, CDSL and KFin on forward earnings and cash flow, with a shallower bear case.

## Why CAMS

1. **Durable oligopoly.** CAMS and KFin dominate MF RTA. CAMS serviced ₹56 lakh crore of MF AUM in Q1 FY27, a 67.2% share. AMC workflows, investor records, distributor mappings and compliance history make switching operationally risky. The moat protects retention; the risk is fee regulation, not volume collapse.
2. **Recurring, asset-linked revenue.** MF is 86% of FY26 revenue; non-MF (14%) grew 28.4% YoY. FY27 guidance is revenue +13%, EBITDA +16%. Q1 FY27 revenue grew 11.5% and PAT 17.3%, with a 46.1% EBITDA margin.
3. **Superior financial quality.** FY26 CFO ₹584 crore, FCF ₹444 crore, FCF/EBITDA 65%, net cash ₹906 crore. Average-equity ROE 38.7%, pre-tax ROCE 42.2%. Dividend payout 65%; no leverage.
4. **Valuation support.** 35.3x TTM, 31.3x FY27E, 27.7x FY28E, 24.0x EV/EBITDA, 2.6% FCF yield. BSE trades at 50.6x TTM/40.3x EV/EBITDA; CDSL at 57.0x/43.1x; KFin at 42.6x/27.2x.

The target is 31.8x FY28E EPS, 2.8% FY27E FCF yield and 21.1x FY28E EV/EBITDA — a modest re-rating from 27.7x, not an aggressive one. DCF is only ₹712 because the terminal value is 71% of EV and WACC is 10%; the target relies on the forward multiple. At a constant 27.7x, the stock is worth ₹697; at 35x, ₹881. The range is ₹712–831; ₹800 is mid-to-upper.

**Scenarios:** Bull ₹969 (+41% total), scenario base ₹831 (+21%), bear ₹532 (−22%); official blended target ₹800. Probability-weighted total return +15.2%.

## Catalysts

- **Q2 FY27 results (29/30 Oct):** revenue growth ≥10%, EBITDA margin ≥45%, MF share ≥65%, non-MF growth >20%.
- **Monthly AMFI AUM:** SIP flows and CAMS-serviced AUM.
- **Q3 FY27 (Jan 2027):** guidance delivery and cash balance.
- **Union Budget (Feb 2027):** capital-gains/TER changes.
- **SEBI/AMFI consultations:** RTA fee cap/common platform risk.

## Risks and bear case

- **Fee regulation (main risk):** a binding RTA fee cap or common platform that cuts MF revenue yield >10% would reduce the target by ~15–25%. Not in the base case.
- **Market drawdown:** a −20% equity market cuts FY27 revenue ~13% and EPS ~14%; target at the base multiple falls to ₹631, bear case ₹532.
- **Share loss:** MF RTA share below 63% for two quarters would lower growth and the multiple.
- **Execution:** CAMS Q2 is not yet available; Q1 revenue growth (11.5%) was below the 13% guidance.
- **Valuation:** DCF is close to the current price; a de-rating to 28x FY28E implies ₹705.

## Exactly three exit/thesis-review triggers

1. **Share loss:** MF RTA share below 63% for two consecutive quarters, or a top-5 AMC publicly migrates a material mandate.
2. **Fee/regulatory impairment:** a binding RTA fee cap/common platform cuts MF revenue yield >10%, or removes the asset-based model.
3. **Valuation/earnings break:** forward P/E >45x with forward revenue growth <8%, or the stock reaches ₹950 with no upward revision to FY28 EPS.

## Deployment

₹500 crore sleeve; target ₹20 crore (4%), hard cap ₹25 crore (5%). Tranches: ₹10 crore now (price ≤₹720), ₹5 crore on Q2 confirmation (revenue growth ≥10%, EBITDA margin ≥45%), ₹5 crore at ≤₹725 or improved FY28 visibility. Build over ~3 days at ≤15% of assumed ₹50 crore ADV. Do not chase above ₹760 before full deployment; pause above ₹800.

## What would change the view

- **CAMS:** share <63%, fee cap, or a large dilutive acquisition.
- **BSE:** regulatory clarity plus a de-rating below 35x FY28E; or sustained >60% derivatives share with no new curbs.
- **KFin:** two quarters of >42% EBITDA margin and >20% international growth at <₹800.
- **CDSL:** 25–30% price correction or a new growth engine.

## Conclusion

CAMS is the best risk-adjusted way to own Indian capital-market growth for the next 12 months. It is not the fastest grower and not a deep-value stock; it is a high-quality franchise at a reasonable forward multiple with a net-cash balance sheet. Buy, build in tranches, target ₹800, and respect the three triggers.
