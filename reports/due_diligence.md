# Due Diligence and Earnings-Quality Review

**Scope:** BSE, CDSL, CAMS, KFin | **Cut-off:** 29 October 2026 | **Sources:** S01–S13, S17–S18

## 1. Executive summary

No reviewed company shows a fatal accounting red flag. CAMS has the cleanest earnings-to-cash profile (pre-tax CFO/EBITDA 110%; after-tax 86%) and the simplest balance sheet. BSE has the highest cash-flow volatility and the most complex regulatory/clearing balances. CDSL's earnings quality is sound but its FY26 profit declined despite revenue growth. KFin's Ascent acquisition increases intangible assets, working-capital intensity and disclosure complexity.

**Open items:** detailed related-party transactions, contingent liabilities, auditor qualifications, lease maturities and segment capital employed have not been line-by-line reconciled to annual-report notes. They are flagged below and should be closed before external publication. This report does not assert that no issue exists; it states what the reviewed disclosures show.

### BSE

- **Cash-flow volatility:** CFO was ₹2,643 crore in FY24, ₹414 crore in FY25 and ₹3,104 crore in FY26. The FY25 dip reflects working-capital/clearing movements, not a revenue collapse, but it shows the cash-flow statement is not a smooth annuity.
- **Other income volatility:** ₹417 crore in FY24 versus ₹24 crore in FY26. The FY24 number included a large non-operating item; the FY26 base is cleaner but makes PBT growth look different.
- **Revenue definition:** the Q1 FY27 investor presentation reports ₹1,706.8 crore revenue and ₹1,186.7 crore EBITDA versus Screener's ₹1,566 crore/₹1,046 crore. The difference must be reconciled (likely other income/segment definition) before using quarterly margin trends. For FY26, BSE's media release reports ₹5,148 crore total revenue (₹4,834 crore from operations + ₹314 crore investment/other income) and ₹3,079 crore operating EBITDA, versus ₹5,124 crore/₹3,480 crore normalized from Screener; the ₹290–400 crore gap is classification, not a restatement, but it makes BSE's margin look ~4 pp higher than the company's operating metric.
- **Clearing balances:** consolidated cash/investments include clearing-corporation/SGF-related balances. The model uses a ₹2,500 crore attributable net-cash haircut, not the gross ₹3,837 crore investments line.
- **Concentration:** transaction charges are ~74% of FY26 revenue and ~85% in Q1 FY27. This is an operating risk, not an accounting red flag, but it magnifies regulatory shocks.

### CDSL

- **Profit decline:** FY26 revenue +5.8% but PAT −13.5%, as employee/technology costs and D&A rose. This is visible in the P&L, not hidden in one-offs, but it shows operating leverage working in reverse.
- **Other income:** ₹93 crore, 15.3% of PBT. A meaningful part of profit is non-operating; a lower interest-rate/investment-return environment would hurt.
- **Investments:** ₹1,487 crore at FY26. Some balances are regulatory/operating; the model uses ₹1,698 crore net cash after a judgemental restricted-balance haircut on total cash/investments.
- **Related-party/regulatory:** CDSL is a market infrastructure institution with multiple SEBI/NSDL/DP relationships. Related-party disclosures and SEBI inspection observations need annual-report verification; none were identified as material in the reviewed summaries.
- **Governance:** promoter holding is 15%; no controlling shareholder. This is a low-promoter structure, not necessarily a red flag, but it raises takeover/board-stability questions.

### CAMS

- **Clean cash conversion:** pre-tax CFO/EBITDA 110% in FY26 (Screener; after-tax 86%), FCF ₹444 crore, net cash. Working capital is low/negative; receivables 14 days.
- **Other income:** ₹51 crore, 8.1% of PBT; not dominant.
- **D&A/leases:** ₹99 crore D&A, 6.5% of revenue. The model assumes D&A at 6.2% of revenue and capex at 6.5%, broadly steady-state. Capitalised software/lease accounting should be checked in the annual report.
- **Payout:** 65% dividend payout; sustainable given FCF, but limits reinvestment and makes the equity story more cash-return dependent.
- **Ownership:** no promoter; FIIs ~45.6%, DIIs ~21.8% (Sep 2026). High institutional ownership improves governance scrutiny but can amplify flows/volatility.
- **Related party:** CAMS has historical relationships with AMCs/shareholders. No material adverse related-party issue was identified in the reviewed materials; the annual-report RPT note remains an open verification item.

### KFin

- **Ascent acquisition:** increases goodwill/intangibles, D&A and working capital. D&A/revenue is 6.6%; debtor days 76, the highest in the group. FCF/EBITDA 49% is the lowest.
- **Margin volatility:** Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin 34%, Q2 FY27 40.1%. The recovery is positive, but the swing shows integration/operating leverage risk.
- **Tax rate:** 27% in FY26; check for one-offs/foreign mix.
- **Promoter sell-down:** promoter holding fell from 22.86% (Mar 2026) to 14.06% (Sep 2026). The stake sale improves free float but raises governance/overhang questions.
- **International complexity:** multiple jurisdictions, currencies and regulators; segment disclosure is less granular than CAMS's. This is a disclosure/complexity risk, not an accounting red flag.

This is the main remaining diligence gap.

## 6. Accounting-judgement watchlist

1. **Revenue recognition** — asset-based RTA fees and transaction charges; check cut-off and accrued revenue.
2. **Capitalised software/intangibles** — CAMS/KFin; ensure capitalisation is not masking operating cost.
3. **Lease accounting** — CAMS/KFin D&A includes lease amortisation; check lease liabilities and maturities.
4. **Clearing/SGF balances** — BSE/CDSL; separate operating cash from restricted balances.
5. **Other income** — CDSL/BSE; isolate non-recurring gains.
6. **Acquisition accounting** — KFin/Ascent; goodwill, intangibles, contingent consideration and earn-outs.
7. **Tax one-offs** — KFin's 27% rate; BSE's FY24/FY25 variations.

## 7. Conclusion

- **CAMS:** cleanest earnings quality, simplest balance sheet, best cash conversion. No fatal red flag; main open items are RPT/lease notes.
- **BSE:** strong cash generation but volatile and complex; revenue-definition inconsistency and clearing balances require reconciliation. Regulatory concentration is the dominant risk.
- **CDSL:** sound cash conversion, but other income and regulated balances reduce the quality of the headline; FY26 profit decline is a warning on operating leverage.
- **KFin:** improving but the most complex; Ascent integration, working capital and promoter sell-down warrant a valuation discount.

**Diligence recommendation:** CAMS passes the review with open items that do not change the thesis. BSE/CDSL/KFin are rejected on valuation/risk, not on fraud/accounting grounds.
